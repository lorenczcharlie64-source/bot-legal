Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of this Discord bot (the "Bot") may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you or your Discord server use the Bot. By using the Bot, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, stop using the Bot and ask a server administrator to remove it.

1. Information the Bot may collect

Depending on which features are used and how the Bot is configured, the Bot may process or store:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, and, where needed for a feature, channel or role IDs.
  • Messages and command activity: command text, command options, relevant message content, and records of commands or interactions. Message content is only handled where needed for a Bot feature or where Discord makes it available to the Bot.
  • Server settings: configuration choices, preferences, and settings saved by server administrators.
  • Logs and moderation records: event logs, moderation actions, reports, and related records where those features are enabled or used.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information to the Bot.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to operate the Bot, respond to commands, remember server settings, provide moderation or logging features, maintain security, investigate abuse or errors, and improve reliability. The Bot should only process information that is reasonably relevant to these purposes.

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and by hosting, database, or other service providers needed to operate the Bot. It may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or others from abuse or security threats. Those providers may handle information under their own privacy terms.

4. Storage and security

Information may be stored on the systems used to host and operate the Bot. Reasonable steps are taken to protect stored information against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing features, maintaining security, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by data type and feature. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or de-identified where reasonably practicable.

You can request access to or deletion of information associated with you or your server by contacting the developer through the Bot's published Discord support account or support channel. The developer may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant records. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Children's privacy

The Bot is not intended to collect more information than is needed to provide its features. Users should follow Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws in their location. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, contact the developer using the support method above.

This policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.
